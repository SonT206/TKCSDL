The rapid development of e-commerce and smart logistics has created an increasing demand for efficient and intelligent last-mile delivery solutions. Drone delivery has emerged as a promising approach for transporting lightweight packages in environments such as smart campuses, industrial parks, residential communities, and remote areas.\\
The SmartDroneDelivery project is proposed as an AI-powered Drone Delivery Management Platform that focuses on managing drone-based delivery operations rather than directly controlling drones. The platform is designed to support the complete delivery lifecycle, including delivery request management, package management, delivery execution, delivery tracking, landing station management, and delivery confirmation. In addition, AI-assisted services are introduced to support estimated delivery time, delivery summarization, customer assistance, and operational analytics.\\
As an important component of the overall system, the database is responsible for organizing and maintaining the data generated throughout these business processes. A well-designed database is essential for ensuring data consistency, integrity, traceability, security, and efficient access for the application layer.\\
This document focuses specifically on the database design of the SmartDroneDelivery platform. The scope includes requirement analysis related to data, identification of entities and relationships, conceptual and logical database design, relational schema design, integrity constraints, indexing, and database implementation using PostgreSQL.\\
