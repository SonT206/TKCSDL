\section{System Overview}\label{s:section}

\qquad The SmartDroneDelivery platform is an AI-powered Drone Delivery Management Platform designed to support the management of drone-based delivery operations. The system focuses on business and logistics management rather than direct drone control.

The platform supports the complete delivery lifecycle, beginning with the creation of a delivery request and continuing through package management, delivery scheduling, delivery execution, delivery tracking, and delivery confirmation.

The system is designed to provide a centralized platform for managing customers, delivery orders, packages, landing stations, drones, delivery activities, and related operational information. AI-assisted services are also introduced to support estimated delivery time, delivery summarization, customer assistance, and operational analytics.

From a database perspective, the system requires a structured and reliable data management layer capable of storing and maintaining information generated throughout these business processes.

\section{Non-functional Requirements}
In addition to functional requirements, the system must satisfy several non-functional requirements that affect database design.
\subsection{Security}
The system shall provide secure authentication and role-based authorization. Database design must support access control and protection of sensitive information.
\subsection{Performance}
The system is expected to provide a response time of less than three seconds for common operations. Appropriate database structures, indexes, and query design should therefore be considered.
\subsection{Concurrency}
The system shall support concurrent users while maintaining stable performance and data consistency.
\subsection{Integration}
The platform shall provide RESTful APIs for integration with external services. The database must therefore provide a consistent and structured data layer for backend services.
\subsection{Scalability and Maintainability}
The database should be designed in a modular and maintainable manner so that additional services and business functions can be introduced in the future.
\subsection{Audit and Backup}
The system shall support system monitoring, audit logging, and backup. The database design must therefore consider the storage of audit information and support reliable data recovery.

\section{Business Processes}
The main business processes supported by the SmartDroneDelivery platform are described below.
\subsection{Delivery Request Process}
The delivery request process begins when a customer creates a delivery order.
The general process is:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Customer creates a delivery request.
    \item Customer provides delivery address and package information.
    \item The system stores the delivery request.
    \item Dispatcher reviews the request.
    \item Dispatcher approves or rejects the request.
\end{enumerate}

This process requires the database to store customer information, delivery address, package information, order information, and order status.

\subsection{Delivery Scheduling Process}
After a delivery request is approved, the delivery can be scheduled.
\subsection{Delivery Execution and Tracking Process}
During delivery execution, the system records the progress of the delivery.
\subsection{Delivery Confirmation Process}
When the package reaches its destination, the delivery must be confirmed.
\subsection{Failed Delivery Process}
A delivery may fail for various operational reasons.

\section{Business Rules}
The following business rules are derived from the proposed business processes and are used as the basis for database design.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A customer can create multiple delivery orders.
    
    \item Each delivery order belongs to one customer.
    
    \item A customer may have multiple delivery addresses.
    
    \item Each delivery order is associated with a delivery address.
    
    \item A delivery order contains package information.
    
    \item A delivery order can have multiple status history records.
    
    \item A delivery order can have multiple tracking records during delivery execution.
    
    \item A delivery order may have one delivery confirmation after successful completion.
    
    \item A drone can be assigned to multiple delivery activities over time.
    
    \item A delivery activity is associated with a specific delivery order.
    
    \item A landing station can support multiple delivery operations depending on its capacity.
    
    \item Station availability may change over time.
    
    \item Delivery tracking records represent historical delivery progress.
    
    \item Delivery information must preserve historical records required for monitoring and auditing.
    
    \item Users may have different roles and permissions according to the responsibilities defined by the system.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Data Requirements}
Based on the system requirements and business rules, the database must manage several major categories of data.
\subsection{User and Authorization Data}
The database must store user account and authorization information required to control access to the system. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item User identification and account information.
    \item Authentication information required for user login.
    \item User roles within the system.
    \item Permissions associated with each role.
    \item User account status.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to identify users and control access to system functions according to their assigned roles and permissions. 
\subsection{Delivery Order Data}
  The database must store delivery order information to support the creation, processing, and management of delivery requests. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Delivery order identification information.
    \item Customer associated with each delivery order.
    \item Delivery address associated with each delivery order.
    \item Order creation date and related delivery information.
    \item Current delivery order status.
    \item Status history records for each delivery order.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to manage delivery orders and preserve the history of changes throughout the delivery process.

\subsection{Package Data}
The database must store package information associated with delivery orders. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Package identification information.
    \item Delivery order associated with the package.
    \item Package description and related information.
    \item Package weight and dimensions.
    \item Package status information.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to manage package information and determine the package details required for drone-based delivery operations.


\subsection{Drone and Delivery Data}
 The database must store drone and delivery activity information required for delivery execution. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Drone identification information.
    \item Drone status and availability information.
    \item Delivery activity information.
    \item Drone assigned to each delivery activity.
    \item Delivery order associated with each delivery activity.
    \item Delivery activity status and time information.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to associate drones with delivery activities and manage delivery execution over time.


\subsection{Landing Station Data}
The database must store information about landing stations used in delivery operations. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Landing station identification information.
    \item Landing station location information.
    \item Station capacity information.
    \item Current station availability.
    \item Delivery operations associated with the landing station.
    \item Historical station availability information when required.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to manage landing stations and determine their availability for delivery operations.


\subsection{Tracking and Confirmation Data}
The database must store tracking and confirmation information generated during and after delivery execution. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Delivery order associated with each tracking record.
    \item Tracking time information.
    \item Delivery progress and status information.
    \item Historical tracking records.
    \item Delivery confirmation information.
    \item Confirmation time and completion status.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to monitor delivery progress, preserve historical tracking information, and record successful delivery confirmation.


\subsection{AI-assisted Service Data}
The database must provide delivery-related data required by AI-assisted services. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Delivery order information.
    \item Package and delivery activity information.
    \item Historical delivery and tracking information.
    \item Delivery time information used for estimation.
    \item Operational data used for analytics.
    \item Information required for delivery summarization and customer assistance.
\end{itemize}

This data supports AI-assisted services such as estimated delivery time, delivery summarization, customer assistance, and operational analytics.


\subsection{Audit Data}

The database must preserve audit information required for monitoring and accountability. The required data includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item User associated with an important system action.
    \item Type of action performed.
    \item Date and time of the action.
    \item Relevant record or operation affected by the action.
    \item Historical information required for monitoring and auditing.
\end{itemize}

This data enables the system to maintain traceability of important operations and support monitoring and auditing activities.
