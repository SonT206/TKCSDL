\section{Conclusion}
Through the design and testing process, the database schema for the drone delivery system 
has been successfully developed. The schema covers all critical aspects of the business workflow:
customers, orders, packages, drones, landing stations, tracking, workflows, confirmations, 
and audit logs. Testing confirmed that the schema maintains referential integrity, supports 
error handling (such as drone replacement), and ensures transparency through audit logging.

The evaluation demonstrated that the database is consistent, reliable, and scalable. 
It can handle multiple drones and stations simultaneously, while providing accountability 
for user actions. Overall, the design meets the operational requirements of a modern drone 
delivery business.

\section{Future Development}
Although the current schema is functional and robust, several improvements can be considered:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{IoT Integration}: Connect real-time telemetry from drones directly into the Tracking table.
    \item \textbf{Advanced Security}: Implement role-based access control and encryption for sensitive data.
    \item \textbf{Analytics}: Extend the schema to support predictive analytics for delivery times and drone maintenance.
    \item \textbf{Customer Feedback}: Add a module for customer reviews and service quality monitoring.
    \item \textbf{Automation}: Integrate automated alerts for lost packages or drone failures to improve responsiveness.
\end{itemize}

These enhancements would increase the system’s resilience, improve customer satisfaction, 
and provide valuable insights for business optimization.
